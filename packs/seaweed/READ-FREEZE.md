# Volume read-freeze: scope and expected outputs

The plan is the accepted M01/M02 read-restart shape with `restart.freeze=true`, role `volume` (or omitted), `recover_http=""`, and the existing `seaweed_read_restart` action. Independent policy supplies `process_pause` with the same episode, the deployed volume label, bounded deadline and `allow_work_resume=true`; generation restarts must be absent. Produce the plan with `MarshalPlan` and the existing ProfileArgs/ReadLoadArgs. There is no new controller or native adapter.

The measured cap required the pre-approved HTTP cut: HTTP before/after controls are DEFERRED for this case. The volume/filer restart cases retain their existing DC+HTTP behavior when freeze is absent. ENOSPC and write-under-fault remain deferred.

The actual approved worker/Engine seeds and settles exact DC keys, starts the unchanged 120 s read-only DC worker, waits 30 s, registers resume custody before STOP and witnesses all threads stopped. After at least 20 s, normal resume joins STOP and the gate, freshly admits WORK, then sends the one CONT. Load continues and must settle with its owned output. Fresh DC GETs at load end must return every seeded byte.

Evidence: same M01 monotonic clock/boot P immediately before STOP, S after its exact-generation STOP witness, C before CONT, and ResumedMono after the exact LIVE witness is validated. Require C-S >=20 s and at least one actual per-GET interval intersecting [S,C]. Missing exposure is NOT_COVERED and cannot produce PASS. At least one load GET must START after ResumedMono; neither a start after the pre-C marker nor a fresh recovery job substitutes for continuing load. Every failed operation starts at or after P; there is at most one contiguous failure window and its first exact completion is <=P+60. Zero wrong bytes always; zero errors is allowed. DC direct counts equal read_objects, RC/copied are zero and fallback is false, as checked by the reused assessor. Named recovery GETs prove bytes, not a second timed recovery claim. No remote wall-clock or DMA-in-flight claim is made.

Normal resume shares only the private grant/latch/CONT/witness primitive with terminal cleanup. WORK cancellation is attached, effect deadline is WORK-bounded, and an observed revoke before arming sends zero normal CONT while retaining terminal cleanup. A revoke/Close after arming joins and classifies the same attempt; no continuing-WORK success or second CONT follows. A valid physical success can still be proved settled after WORK closes. Lost replies/panics/failed CONT stay UNKNOWN, never rehabilitated by a later LIVE sample. The non-STOP signal still only checks readback and does not promise instantaneous remote revocation.

Close order remains: close admission and join STOP/armed CONT before any OTHER settlement (UNKNOWN join returns without settling other targets); settle other issuers/load; fresh victim LIVE; victim settlement; older callbacks. A successful normal CONT is cleared but not final and skips the second CONT. Final proof and all existing hard bounds remain required. Historical native victim-liveness refusal remains OPEN/UNKNOWN; this code does not resolve it. Local fake/owned-pidfd controls are not lab qualification.
