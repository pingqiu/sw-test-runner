- Pack EC assessment from frozen manifest + submitted/terminal journal + fault/heal receipts + optional product witness. Catches wrong bytes, omitted/duplicate operations, wrong route/status/generation/interval and post-heal attempt or completion; does not prove the producer told the truth.
